### 2\. Information We Collect

We collect information necessary to provide and improve our Services.

#### A. Merchant Information

When merchants install Custy, we may collect:

- Store name and domain
- Contact details (email, billing info)
- Account settings and preferences
- Subscription and billing data
- Support communications

#### B. Shopify API Data

Through Shopify APIs, we may access:

- Product data (titles, variants, images, options)
- Order data (items, selections, fulfillment info)
- Customer data (name, email, shipping details as required)
- Store configuration data

#### C. Customization & Design Data

Custy processes:

- Uploaded images, logos, artwork
- Custom text entered by customers
- Design positions, layers, colors, sizes
- Print areas (front, back, sleeves, etc.)
- Generated previews and print-ready files

#### D. Automatically Collected Data

We may collect:

- IP address
- Device and browser information
- Usage data and logs
- Cookies and tracking data
