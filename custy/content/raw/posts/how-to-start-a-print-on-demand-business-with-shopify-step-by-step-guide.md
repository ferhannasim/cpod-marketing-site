Starting a print-on-demand (POD) business is one of the easiest ways to enter eCommerce with low risk and high scalability.

### Step 1: Choose Your Niche

Pick a niche like:

- Fitness
- Pets
- Gaming
- Funny quotes

Niche stores convert better than general stores.

### Step 2: Set Up Shopify Store

Create your Shopify store and choose a clean theme. Focus on:

- Mobile optimization
- Fast loading
- Simple navigation

### Step 3: Add Customization

This is where you stand out.

Using Custy, you can:

- Let customers design their own products
- Offer multiple print areas
- Increase engagement

### Step 4: Connect Fulfillment

Use print providers like:

- Printful
- Printify
- Local suppliers

### Step 5: Launch & Market

Start with:

- Facebook Ads
- TikTok Ads
- Influencer marketing

### 🎯 Conclusion

With the right tools and strategy, you can build a scalable POD business quickly. Custy gives you a competitive edge by turning simple products into personalized experiences.
