![](/images/content/steps.png)

Step 1: Select the product you wish to print on from our vast selection  
Step 2: Choose your favourite color for the product you picked  
Step 3: Upload your design using our Easy to Use Design Studio or choose from our immense selection of over 3000 designs!  
Step 4: Pick the size(s) and amount for each size you wish to order.  
Step 5: Click add to cart! You can checkout now or continue to shop for more products.  
  
If you are having any difficulty during any of these steps, please give us a call- our friendly staff is always eager and ready to help
