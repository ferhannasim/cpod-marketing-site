**Disclaimer:**

This guide is intended for reference only. While we do our best to ensure consistent sizing across all products, some styles may vary. Our garment measurements align with the size charts provided by our suppliers, allowing for a potential variance of up to 1 inch (smaller or larger).

For best results, follow the care instructions on the garment's laundry tag. **DropShipPOD** is not responsible for any damage caused by improper care.

**How to measure:**  
**WIDTH:** Lay garment flat. 1" below the armhole, measure the garment across the chest.  
**LENGTH:** Lay garment flat (face down). Measure from center back neckline seam straight down to the bottom of the front hem.  
**SLEEVE LENGTH:** Lay garment flat (face down). Measure from center back neck to outer edge of shoulder seam, then along the outer edge to the sleeve end.
