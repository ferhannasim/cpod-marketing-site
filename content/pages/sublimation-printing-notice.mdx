## ⚠️ IMPORTANT NOTICE – SUBLIMATION PRINTING (PLEASE READ BEFORE ORDERING)

Sublimation printing is a **lower-grade printing method** compared to our premium **DTF (Direct to Film)** printing.

By selecting sublimation printing, you acknowledge and accept the following:

### 🔹 Print Quality & Appearance

- Sublimation produces a **significantly lighter, faded, and less vibrant print** compared to DTF.

- Colors may appear **washed out, dull, or less saturated**, especially on lighter fabrics.

- Sublimation **does NOT produce solid, bold, or opaque colors**.


### 🔹 Fabric Limitations (VERY IMPORTANT)

- Sublimation ONLY works on **light-colored polyester garments**.

- It does **NOT work properly on cotton, dark garments, or blended fabrics**.

- The print **bonds into the fibers**, meaning:

    - It may appear **uneven depending on fabric texture**

    - It may show **fabric grain, stretch marks, or inconsistencies**


### 🔹 Design Limitations

- Fine details, small text, and sharp edges may appear **blurred or less defined**.

- There is **NO white ink in sublimation**, so any white areas in your design will be **transparent (showing garment color)**.


### 🔹 Expectation vs. Reality

- The final result will **NOT match digital mockups or screen colors**.

- Sublimation is intended for **basic, low-cost printing only** — not premium-quality output.
