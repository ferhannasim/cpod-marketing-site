DropShipPOD started as a Direct-to-Film (DTF) print shop, and DTF is still the backbone of our production floor: vivid colour, a soft hand-feel, and prints that hold up wash after wash on cotton, blends, dark garments, and just about anything else DTF touches.

### Why sellers work with us

- **Print quality first.** High-grade DTF film and inks give every print vibrant colour and crisp detail, on fabric that other transfer methods can't always reach.
- **Fast turnaround.** Most orders are processed and shipped within 24-72 hours — one of the faster production cycles in the industry.
- **No minimums.** One custom piece or a full production run, we print it the same way: on demand, to order. Rush production is available when a deadline is tight.
- **Straightforward pricing.** What you're quoted is what you pay, with no surprise fees buried in the invoice.

## What we run

DropShipPOD is three things working together:

- **Print production.** Our own floor runs DTF, including transfer sheets for anyone who wants to press their own designs. DTG, sublimation and embroidery are available through the wider catalog for products DTF doesn't fit — drinkware, all-over prints, and stitched logos.
- **The DropShipPOD Shopify app.** The platform sellers use to import products, set their markup, and route every order to production automatically, with no inventory to carry.
- **[Custy](/custy).** Our sibling app for on-storefront personalization — shoppers add text, cliparts and their own uploads before an order ever reaches us, and it lands print-ready in the same production pipeline.

## How we work

Every order is produced in Canada, printed per order — nothing goes to press until a customer buys it. That means no warehouse of dead stock sitting on shelves, no size or colour run going stale before it sells, and no guesswork about what to keep on hand. What you or your customer receives was made for that specific order, not pulled from a bin that's been sitting since last season.
