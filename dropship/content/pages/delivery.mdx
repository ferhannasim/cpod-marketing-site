Every order is produced here in Canada, then handed to a courier for delivery across the country. Here's what to expect at each stage, and what to do if something looks off along the way.

## Production time

We aim to fulfill every order — printing, pressing and packing — within 1-4 business days of it being placed. Complex artwork, larger volumes or an address correction can push that by a day; we'll email you if yours is going to run past this window.

## Shipping across Canada

Once an order leaves our facility, transit time depends on where it's headed.

**Ground shipping**

- ON, QC, NB: 1-2 business days
- MB, SK, NS, NL, PE: 2-4 business days
- AB, BC: 3-5 business days

**Express shipping**

- All provinces: 1-2 business days

Ground shipping is billed at a flat rate by region:

- ON, QC, NB, NS, NL, PE: $14.99 CAD
- AB, SK, MB: $19.99 CAD
- BC: $24.99 CAD

Orders over $199 CAD ship free, anywhere in the country, and local pickup is available too — choose "Pick-Up" as your shipping method at checkout to collect your order from our Dollard, QC warehouse instead of waiting on a courier. Express rates are calculated at checkout once you've entered an address.

We don't currently ship to the Northwest Territories, Yukon or Nunavut.

**Secure shipping — $5.99 CAD**

Add signature-required delivery to any order: the courier won't leave the package unless someone signs for it. Select "Secure Shipping" at checkout. It's worth adding whenever a lost or stolen package would be hard to make right before your customer notices.

## Tracking

A tracking number is generated the moment your order ships and syncs automatically to the Shopify order — no separate lookup, no manual entry. You'll see it on the order in your Shopify admin as soon as the courier scans the package.

## Delays & questions

Weather, customs and courier volume can occasionally push a delivery past its estimate, especially around holidays. If a package is running late, or you need to change an address after production has already started, [contact us](/contact) and we'll look into it. For everything else — pickup, packaging, what happens if a shipment goes missing — check the [FAQ](/faq) first; most delivery questions are already answered there.
