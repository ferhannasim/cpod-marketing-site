# Free Shipping on Orders Over $199

We want to make high quality custom apparel accessible to everyone. That is why we have **FREE SHIPPING ON ORDERS OVER $199** in Canada. No matter where you live, we at Cheapest Print on Demand will make sure your order gets to you fast. Below, you will find our shipping options.

- **Pick-up in-warehouse**: Our warehouse is located in at 72-C Bruswick, Dollard, QC, H9B 2C5. You are more than welcome to pick-up your online order in-warehouse. We are open 7 days a week. Simply place your order online and choose “Pick-Up” as your carrier for warehouse pick-up.
    

- **Free Shipping**: We offer FREE SHIPPING ON ORDERS OVER $199 across North America using Purolator, GLS or FedEx. We will provide a tracking number on all orders. Please note that  Cheapest Print on Demand is **NOT RESPONSIBLE** under **ANY CIRCUMSTANCES** for any shipping delays, package theft or shipping mishaps. Shipping on orders under $199 is a flat rate of just $14.99 per order if shipped to **ON, QC, NB, NS, NL, PE,**  if your order is being shipped to **AB, SK, MB** \= $19.99 and if your order is being shipped to **BC** \= $24.99
    

- **Secure Shipping**: For a small flat fee of $5.99, you can have secure shipping. This means your package will not be delivered unless someone signs for it. If you prefer this method of delivery, please select "Secure Shipping" option at checkout.

Please review and select one option before completing your order:
