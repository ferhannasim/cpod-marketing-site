# 🎨 What Are DTF Transfers?

**DTF (Direct to Film)** is a cutting-edge print method that lets you create vibrant, long-lasting designs on almost any fabric. It’s the ultimate way to get pro-quality prints — without the hassle.

Here’s how it works:  
Your design is printed onto a special film using eco-friendly inks. A powdered adhesive is applied and cured, creating a ready-to-press transfer. Then you simply heat press it onto your fabric of choice.

✅ Vivid color, high detail  
✅ Works on dark and light fabrics

✅ Long-lasting + flexible finish

# 🔥 How to Apply DTF Transfers

Whether you’ve got a heat press or just a household iron — here’s how to get a clean, durable finish:

* * *

## 🛠️ With a Heat Press (Best Results)

**1\. Preheat Your Press**

Set to **300°F** with **medium to firm pressure**.

**2\. Place the Transfer**

Design facing down on the fabric, use heat tape if needed to hold the transfer sheet in place. Cover with heat press paper or Teflon.

**3\. First Press**

Press for 20 **seconds**.

**4\. Cool & Peel**

Let the film **cool for 5-10 seconds** before peeling. If your design includes fine details, we recommend allowing a minimum of **30 seconds** before peeling. Gently remove the film to ensure the best result. Repress if needed.

**5\. Final Press**

Cover again and press for **another 20 seconds** to seal and smooth the print.

* * *

## 🔥 Using a Household Iron

**1\. Heat It Up**  
Set to the **hottest setting** (cotton/linen). **No steam**.

**2\. Place the Design**  
Position the film and tape it if needed. Cover with parchment or a silicone sheet.

**3\. Press in Sections**  
Press firmly for **8–10 seconds** per section. Don’t slide the iron — press and hold.

**4\. Peel & Press Again**  
Wait a few seconds, then peel. If parts lift, press again. Finish with a second press using parchment.

# 🧼 Garment Care Instructions

Want your designs to last? Follow these simple tips:

- Wait **24 hours** before first wash
    
- Wash **inside out, cold water, gentle cycle**
    
- **Low heat** in the dryer (or hang dry for best results)
    
- Avoid harsh detergents or bleach
    

Properly applied and cared for, our DTF transfers can last **60+ washes** or **2–5 years**! 💪
